1. Shipping & Delivery Questions (#shipping-faq)

Q: How long does delivery take across India?
A: Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days, while Express Air delivery takes 1–2 business days. Full details are on our Shipping & Delivery Policy page.
Q: Is there a minimum order value for free shipping?
A: Yes, all orders above ₹999 ($25.00) qualify for free doorstep shipping.

2. Returns & Refunds Questions (#return-faq)

Q: What is your return window?
A: You may request a replacement or full refund within 30 calendar days of delivery under our Return & Refund Policy.

3. Account & Privacy Questions (#account-faq)

Q: Where can I view or export my personal data stored on this website?
A: Visit the My Data on This E-Commerce Website page to inspect or manage your stored account records.
